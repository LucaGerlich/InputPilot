## InputPilot 1.1.0

**After updating, the welcome window appears once** so you can turn on *Open InputPilot at login*. Without it, InputPilot stops working after a restart.

### New
- **Layouts you pick yourself are kept.** Switch with ⌃Space, the input menu or Undo, and InputPilot keeps your choice until you type on another keyboard. Settings → Switching offers alternatives, including the old behaviour.
- **Open at login**, in the welcome window and in Settings.
- **"Use … for …"** in the menu maps the keyboard you are typing on to the current input source in one click.
- **Relaunch InputPilot** finishes setup after you grant Input Monitoring.

### Fixed
- Two USB keyboards of the same model, on different ports, now each get their own mapping.
- Karabiner-Elements holding a keyboard no longer stops keyboard detection outright.
- Emoji & Symbols and Dictation are no longer offered as mapping targets.
- Settings and other windows now open in front of the active app.
- A lone F6 press now counts as typing.
